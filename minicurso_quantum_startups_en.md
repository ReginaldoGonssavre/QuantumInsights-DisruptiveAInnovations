# Mini-course: Quantum for Startups: How to Apply in Practice

## Course Outline (3 Videos)

### Module 1: Demystifying Quantum Computing for Startups (Video 1)

**Objective:** Introduce basic quantum computing concepts in an accessible way and show why startups should care.

*   **Introduction (2 min)**
    *   Welcome and what the student will learn.
    *   Why quantum computing is not just for large corporations.
*   **What is Quantum Computing? (5 min)**
    *   Simple analogy: classical bits vs. qubits (superposition and entanglement).
    *   Exponential processing power (without going into mathematical details).
*   **Myths and Realities (3 min)**
    *   Not meant to replace classical computers, but to solve specific problems.
    *   Not science fiction, it's already a reality (examples from companies).
*   **Opportunities for Startups (5 min)**
    *   Optimization (logistics, finance, manufacturing).
    *   Simulation (new materials, drugs).
    *   Cryptography (data security).
*   **Free Resources to Get Started (3 min)**
    *   Platforms like IBM Quantum Experience, Qiskit.
    *   Introductory online courses.
*   **Call to Action (2 min)**
    *   Invitation to the next module.
    *   Link to the free e-book.

### Module 2: Identifying Quantum-Solvable Problems in Your Startup (Video 2)

**Objective:** Help the student identify real problems in their startup that can benefit from quantum computing.

*   **Introduction (1 min)**
    *   Quick review of Module 1.
*   **Thinking Quantum: Types of Problems (7 min)**
    *   Optimization problems (e.g., delivery routing, resource allocation).
    *   Simulation problems (e.g., new product development, chemical reactions).
    *   Machine learning problems (e.g., complex pattern recognition).
*   **Simplified Case Studies (8 min)**
    *   Logistics startup: optimizing routes with QAOA.
    *   Finance startup: optimizing portfolios with quantum annealing.
    *   Biotech startup: simulating molecules for drug discovery.
*   **Practical Exercise (5 min)**
    *   Guides the student to think about 1-2 problems in their own startup.
    *   Key questions for self-assessment.
*   **Tools for Analysis (2 min)**
    *   How to use quantum simulators to test initial ideas.
*   **Call to Action (2 min)**
    *   Invitation to the next module.
    *   Offer quick consulting for case analysis.

### Module 3: First Steps and Future Challenges (Video 3)

**Objective:** Guide the student on how to take the first practical steps and what to expect in the future of quantum computing.

*   **Introduction (1 min)**
    *   Quick review of Module 2.
*   **Building a Quantum Team (5 min)**
    *   No need for a quantum physicist initially.
    *   Collaboration with universities and research centers.
    *   Hiring problem-solving oriented talent.
*   **Platforms and APIs (7 min)**
    *   Quick demonstration of how to run a simple circuit in Qiskit (without complex code).
    *   Focus on the ease of use of APIs.
*   **Challenges and the Near Future (5 min)**
    *   Evolving Hardware: IBM's "Starling" and the Quest for Stability (200 qubits by 2029).
    *   The Importance of Hybrid Algorithms (Classical-Quantum) and the Convergence with AI.
    *   Investment Opportunities, Partnerships, and the Potential of Quantum AI with Quantum Energy.
*   **Advanced Resources (3 min)**
    *   Online communities, conferences, acceleration programs.
*   **Conclusion and Final Call to Action (3 min)**
    *   Reaffirm the technology's potential.
    *   Invitation for full consulting and to connect on LinkedIn.
    *   Thanks.
